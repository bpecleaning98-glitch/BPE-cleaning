Brand pack from the designer (BPE - Cleaning Services.zip, 16 Aug 2026).

Kept here as the source of truth for the identity. The site itself does NOT
import from this folder: scripts/build-marks.mjs converts the two-tone SVGs
into src/data/marks.ts, and src/components/brand/BpeLogo.astro renders them.

Naming, from the designer's export:
  LPO = Logo principal, orizontal   (symbol + BPE + CLEANING SERVICES, in a row)
  LSV = Logo secundar, vertical     (symbol above the name)
  LST = Logo secundar, tipografic   (name only, no symbol)
  S   = Simbol                      (the diamond mark alone)
Colour suffixes: ALB = white, NEGRU = black, AURIU = gold, and the pairs.

Palette (brand book p.4): ink #222223, gold #C59A4A, paper #ECEBEC.
Type (brand book p.6): Avenir for display, Fort for text.

The full pack, including AI/EPS/PDF masters, mockups and the brand guidelines
PDF (87 MB), stays in the designer's zip, not in this repo.
