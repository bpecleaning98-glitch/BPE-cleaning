This article explains what you are paying for, what moves the price up, and when an end of tenancy clean costs less than the deposit deduction it is meant to prevent.

## Why end of tenancy cleaning costs more than a regular clean

A regular clean maintains a home that is already in reasonable shape. An end of tenancy clean has to return a property to the standard an inventory clerk, a letting agent or a landlord will inspect room by room, often with the check-in photographs beside them.

That means every surface, not just the visible ones:

- inside the oven, hob, extractor and fridge;
- inside kitchen cupboards and drawers, which are now empty;
- limescale on taps, shower screens, tiles and the toilet;
- skirting boards, door frames, light switches and sockets;
- inside the windows, sills and frames;
- under and behind anything that was moved out;
- floors hoovered and mopped edge to edge, including under the bed position.

In an empty property there is nothing to work around, which is why a fixed price per size makes sense. The size of the property is a far better predictor of the time involved than how it looks at a glance.

Two of these matter more than people expect at move-out. The oven is the first thing many inspectors open, and a carpet that has been lived on for two years rarely passes on hoovering alone. If the tenancy agreement mentions professional carpet cleaning, keep the receipt: it is often the only proof that will be accepted.

## What can make it cheaper

A few things keep an end of tenancy clean at the standard price rather than pushing it into an individual quote:

1. **Move out first.** The team cleans an empty property faster and better. Boxes in the hallway and bags on the kitchen floor slow everything down.
2. **Clear the fridge and cupboards.** Food left behind has to be dealt with before cleaning can begin.
3. **Finish any repairs beforehand.** Filler dust, paint splashes and sawdust turn a move-out clean into an [after builders clean](/after-builders-cleaning-dublin/), which is priced differently.
4. **Say what you know.** If the shower screen has heavy limescale or the oven has not been cleaned in a year, tell us. Nobody is judging; it just helps us send the right team for the right amount of time.

## Is it worth paying for?

Compare the cost with what is at stake. In Dublin a deposit is usually one month's rent, and for a two bedroom apartment that is often well above €2,000. A €320 clean that protects it is cheap insurance. Even a partial deduction for cleaning will usually cost more than the clean itself, because the landlord will pay a company to do the work and pass the invoice on.

There is also the time. A thorough move-out clean of a two bedroom apartment takes a team of two most of a day with professional equipment. Done alone with household products, it takes a weekend you are probably spending on the actual move.

Not every tenancy needs it. If you are leaving a well kept studio after a year and the inventory was basic, a serious day of your own cleaning may be enough. ## Landlords and letting agents

The same prices apply when the property is being prepared for the next tenant. Many Dublin landlords book the clean between tenancies directly, so the standard is consistent and the check-in photographs are taken on a clean property. It makes the next move-out inspection fairer for everyone.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does an end of tenancy clean take?

Roughly three to four hours for a one bedroom, five to eight hours for a three bedroom house, with a team of two. The time is ours to manage; the price does not change if it runs long.

### Do you guarantee the deposit?

No cleaning company can promise what a landlord will decide, and the deposit also depends on damage, rent and bills. What we do is clean to the inspection standard, and if something on the checklist is missed, we come back and put it right.

### Should I be there?

You can hand over keys and leave. Many customers arrange access with the agent so the clean happens after the van has gone and before the inspection.
