# Stock imagery

One file lives here, and it is deliberately kept apart from `src/assets/work/`.

`hero-living-room.jpg` is a stock interior photograph, used purely as atmosphere in three
places: the homepage hero, the full-bleed band on the Airbnb page, and the backdrop behind
the contact form. It is never presented as a BPE job.

Everything in `src/assets/work/` is the opposite: real photographs of real BPE jobs, taken by
the team at handover. That is the claim the site makes on the About page and next to the
galleries, and it stays true only as long as the two folders never mix. **A stock frame must
never be moved into `work/`, and must never appear in a gallery, a before/after pair or
anywhere captioned as a job.**
