# Generated imagery

Eleven frames live here, one for the header of each interior page. They are
**AI generated**, not photographs of anything, and they exist for one reason:
every page other than the homepage opened on a bare night band with an empty
right half and, on a phone, nothing above the fold but type.

They are atmosphere. They are never presented as a BPE job.

## The rule this folder shares with `../stock/`

`src/assets/work/` holds real photographs of real BPE jobs, taken by the team
at handover. That is the claim the About page and the galleries make, and it
stays true only as long as the folders never mix.

**Nothing in here may be moved into `work/`, appear in a gallery, appear in a
before-and-after pair, or be captioned as a job we did.** Alt text describes
the room and nothing else: no "our work", no "a clean we finished".
